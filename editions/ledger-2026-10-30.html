Page One · The Big Story

Cannabis Stocks Get Swept Into Wall Street's "AI Reckoning" — MSOS Sheds 5.4% as Vireo and Planet 13 Lead a Broad Rout, the DEA Tells Its Own Judge the GAO Report Deserves "No Weight," and Cresco's Delaware Vote Goes Undisclosed

A violent, largely macro-driven selloff hit the whole sector Friday — not a single cannabis-specific headline — compounding a six-week overhang from the stalled federal rescheduling case. Underneath it, the DEA's long-awaited brief landed, Cresco Labs held its make-or-break corporate-structure vote, and the result still isn't public.

The AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (MSOS) fell 5.39% to $3.86 Friday afternoon on volume of roughly 3.54 million shares, its sharpest one-day drop in recent weeks, as a broader "AI reckoning" selloff tore through U.S. equities on the back of Amazon, Alphabet and Intel earnings reactions — the Nasdaq fell as much as 3% intraday and the S&P 500 slipped alongside it. (Yahoo Finance; TheStreet.) Cannabis names fell harder than the tape: Planet 13 dropped 14.49% to $0.072, Vireo Growth fell 12.03% to $6.95, Cresco Labs lost 6.43% to $0.5894, and Trulieve — now trading on the NYSE as TRLV after its June uplisting — fell 5.57% to $10.01. Verano, TerrAscend, Glass House Brands and Curaleaf all traded down 2-4%, while Aurora Cannabis eased a more modest 1.47% to $4.02. No single new cannabis-specific catalyst explains Friday's magnitude; trade coverage (Tickeron, New Cannabis Ventures) ties the broader weakness to the six-week-old DEA rescheduling-case stay, which has already pulled MSOS down roughly 25% over the trailing month, with acquisitive, debt-funded names like Vireo and Planet 13 — mid-merger with each other — taking the sharpest hits.

Underneath the tape, the day's two substantive developments both involve unresolved process rather than resolved news. Supplemental briefs addressing the admitted GAO report (GAO-26-108623) were in fact filed Thursday, Oct. 29, before ALJ Derek Julius: the DEA itself argued the report deserves "no weight," calling it "not probative to the instant rescheduling analysis" since the underlying scheduling factors were public for two years before the hearing closed. Three opposing filings — from NDASA/DUID Victim Voices/Dr. Kenneth Finn, from SAM with Idaho, Indiana and Nebraska, and from the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation — argued the opposite, that GAO's findings undercut HHS's methodology and that DEA skipped required statutory analysis. Reply briefs are due Nov. 5, with the broader case still formally stayed. (Marijuana Moment; The Marijuana Herald.) Separately, Cresco Labs' shareholders met at noon CDT today to vote on the board-recommended Delaware "TopCo" redomicile — the plan would create a new parent company, move its corporate home from British Columbia to Delaware, and extend the multiple-voting-share sunset to the third anniversary of a U.S. listing. As of this edition's compilation, no results filing had appeared on SEC EDGAR or Cresco's investor site; the board had unanimously recommended all three resolutions.

Why it matters to PGP Friday's selloff is a useful stress test for separating beta from credit risk inside the sector: a market-wide, non-cannabis-specific shock (the AI-capex reassessment) hit every MSO, but it hit hardest the names already carrying deal-related leverage or integration risk — Vireo and Planet 13 mid-merger, Cresco mid-redomicile. PGP's financing and fairness-opinion work should price idiosyncratic risk premiums for clients in active restructurings separately from the sector-wide multiple compression that days like this one produce, rather than letting one explain the other.

The Rescheduling Docket

The DEA tells its own judge the GAO report deserves "no weight"

Supplemental briefs were filed Thursday, Oct. 29, as scheduled. The DEA argued the admitted GAO report is "not probative"; three opponent filings argued the opposite. Reply briefs are due Nov. 5, with the broader Schedule I-to-III case still formally stayed.

  • Apr 23, 2026 DOJ order moves medical / FDA-approved marijuana to Schedule III; encourages Treasury to weigh 280E.

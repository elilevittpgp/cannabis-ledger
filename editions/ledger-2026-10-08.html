MSOS Slides to $4.275 as Glass House Craters 15.6% With No Identified Catalyst — Five Days Before the DEA's Oct. 13 Deadline

The sector's benchmark ETF presses to fresh multi-week lows with no single corporate catalyst behind the move. Separately, the fight over Massachusetts' Question 8 just escalated to two sitting U.S. senators.

The AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (NYSE Arca: MSOS) traded at $4.275 Thursday afternoon (roughly 3:38pm ET), down 4.36% on the day — a fresh leg lower from Wednesday's $4.43 close, confirmed independently on both stockanalysis.com and Yahoo Finance. A verified 1-week or month-to-date change was not available from either source this run, so this desk is not carrying forward last week's approximate "-13%" figure without a fresh print. The standout mover was Glass House Brands, now trading on the NYSE under GLAS (it uplisted June 30, 2026, superseding the OTCQX ticker GLASF this desk previously used — noted below), which plunged roughly 15.6% intraday with no company release, filing, or news item found to explain it.

Separately, the political fight over Massachusetts' Nov. 3 repeal vote gained its highest-profile opponents yet: Sens. Elizabeth Warren and Ed Markey publicly announced opposition to Question 8, with Markey citing new polling showing 75% of voters opposed — up from the 61% figure this desk cited Oct. 7. Gov. Healey separately reiterated her "No" position on WCVB's "On the Record." On the federal docket, state cannabis regulators (including an official in Georgia) are now publicly pressing the DEA for implementation guidance on the rescheduling rollout, with the agency's response to the motion to reopen the stayed case still due Oct. 13 — five days out.

Why it matters to PGP A broad, catalyst-free sell-off with one outlier name moving 15+ points in a day is a volatility signal, not a fundamentals signal — but it still resets the entry price on every name in the comp set five days ahead of a binary federal catalyst. Separately, two sitting U.S. senators publicly opposing a state ballot measure elevates Massachusetts' Question 8 from a local curiosity to a national-attention risk; any PGP mandate with Massachusetts exposure should treat the Nov. 3 vote as a live diligence item regardless of how favorable the polling currently looks.

The Rescheduling Docket

Five days to the DEA's Oct. 13 response — and the politics keep moving

No DEA filing or ALJ ruling has surfaced since the Sept 28-29 stay. The week's actual movement has been political — in Massachusetts, and in states pressing the DEA for guidance it has not yet given.

  • Apr 23, 2026 DOJ order moves medical / FDA-approved marijuana to Schedule III; encourages Treasury to weigh 280E.
  • Jul 15, 2026 Broader Schedule I→III hearings conclude before ALJ Derek Julius.
  • Sep 28-29, 2026 ALJ Julius stays the case pending a GAO report on DEA/FDA scheduling procedures; DEA's response due Oct 13.
  • Sep 29, 2026 Treasury/IRS add Section 280E guidance to the 2026-27 Priority Guidance Plan — a 12-month work window, no completion date or relief amount set.
  • Oct 6-8, 2026 State regulators, including an official in Georgia, publicly press the DEA for implementation guidance on the rescheduling rollout as the Oct 13 deadline approaches.
  • Oct 8, 2026 Sens. Warren and Markey publicly oppose Massachusetts' Question 8 repeal vote, citing new polling showing 75% opposed (up from 61%); Gov. Healey reiterates her "No" position.

What's Next on the Docket

  1. Oct 13, 2026 — DEA's response to the motion to reopen the record (GAO report) is due.
  2. ALJ Julius rules on whether to admit the GAO report and permit added briefing.
  3. If granted, a further briefing round precedes the recommended decision; if denied, the case resumes toward a decision on the existing record.
  4. A 20-day exceptions window, then the DEA Administrator's final call — sell-side analysts have modeled 1H 2027 as the more likely resolution window.
  5. Nov 3, 2026 — Massachusetts voters decide Question 8, which would end licensed adult-use sales as of Jan 1, 2028 if it passes; 75% of surveyed voters currently oppose it per Sen. Markey's cited figure.
  6. Separately: the SAFE Banking Act of 2026 (S.4942) remains referred to the Senate Banking Committee with no markup or floor vote scheduled.
PGP takeaway The 280E guidance project now has a formal federal work-year attached to it (through Sept 30, 2027) but no deadline — treat any model assuming near-term 280E relief as aggressive. Massachusetts' escalating "No on 8" coalition (two senators, the governor) is a reassuring data point but not a reason to skip stress-testing MA-exposed MSOs against a Jan 1, 2028 wind-down scenario.

States press the DEA for rescheduling guidance it hasn't given

State cannabis regulators, including an official in Georgia, are publicly asking the DEA for implementation guidance on the Schedule III rollout, with the agency's formal response to the stayed case still due Oct 13. Marijuana Moment

Spherex becomes vertically integrated with its first-ever acquisition

The Colorado brand bought a Denver-area indoor grow and a Trinidad outdoor cultivation site from The Cannabist Company's Colorado operations, absorbing roughly 60 employees on undisclosed terms. Forbes · Cannabis Business Times

Washington advances a medical-cannabis "verification certificate"

The state's Liquor and Cannabis Board voted unanimously to begin rulemaking on a Medical Cannabis Verification Certificate, overriding its own staff's negative recommendation, aimed at helping licensees document medical-system participation for banking and insurance access. The Marijuana Herald

A third New Jersey dispensary sues over the state's labor-peace mandate

The Cannabis Place 420 Corp. filed a federal suit against the NJ Cannabis Regulatory Commission and UFCW Local 360, arguing the labor-peace-agreement requirement is preempted by the National Labor Relations Act — the third such NJ operator challenge. Hudson County View · MJBizDaily

Treasury and IRS put 280E guidance on next year's priority plan

The Sept 29 Priority Guidance Plan commits federal resources to Section 280E cannabis-tax guidance through Sept 30, 2027, but sets no completion date or relief amount — a work commitment, not a deadline. The Marijuana Herald

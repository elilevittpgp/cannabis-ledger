MSOS Nears a 10% Week as the Calendar Fills With Housekeeping — While a Repeal Vote Emerges in Massachusetts

Green Thumb and Verano file routine Q3 earnings-date notices and Curaleaf lands a second Canadian index inclusion, but the real new thread is organized opposition forming to a ballot measure that would unwind Massachusetts' adult-use market.

The AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (MSOS) traded around $5.41 early Wednesday afternoon, up roughly 1.41% on the session and now higher by roughly 9-10% over the trailing week (derived from a $4.92 close a week ago) — an extension of Tuesday's sharp, fight-driven rally rather than a fresh catalyst. Actual corporate news across the desk's basket was thin and largely administrative: Green Thumb Industries and Verano Holdings each filed routine notices setting Q3 2026 earnings dates (Green Thumb on Nov. 4, Verano on Oct. 29 — Verano's first release of any kind in more than three weeks), and Curaleaf was added to the FTSE Canada All Cap Index, its second major Canadian benchmark inclusion after last year's S&P/TSX Composite addition. That came even as Curaleaf's hostile bid for Aurora Cannabis remains stalled without a hearing date at the Alberta Securities Commission, more than five weeks after Curaleaf's application to halt Aurora's ATM share program. Trading was far calmer than Tuesday's double-digit, fight-driven moves: Curaleaf added a modest 2.50% intraday against Tuesday's close, while Aurora slipped 1.17%.

The priority desk stayed close to dark otherwise. Ascend Wellness's reverse-split ratio remains unset 26 days after stockholder approval, with no new release found despite two separate IR-page fetch attempts (both timed out). Premier Group/PCF's six California license entities were re-checked against the CA Department of Cannabis Control database and again show clean, active status, with no Modesto Bee, Turlock Journal or Santa Cruz Sentinel item in the window. The one genuinely new development sits outside the desk's usual names: the Worcester Regional Chamber of Commerce came out Tuesday against Massachusetts' Question 8, a ballot measure that would repeal the state's legal recreational market outright — one of as many as six marijuana- or psychedelics-related measures that could reach state ballots on Nov. 3, 2026. It is the most direct legal threat this desk has tracked to one of the Northeast's largest legal cannabis markets, where several watchlist names hold retail and cultivation footprints.

Why it matters to PGP Today's tape moves are calendar noise, not signal — two Q3 earnings-date notices and an index inclusion don't change any deal team's model. The item worth flagging to clients is Question 8: a market-repeal ballot measure is a materially different risk than the usual regulatory friction this desk tracks, and any diligence on Massachusetts-exposed operators should now carry an explicit scenario for a Nov. 3 rollback. Separately, Advanced Flower Capital's foreclosure auction of Justice Grown's Pennsylvania collateral is now tomorrow (Sept. 24) — the clearest live read this desk will get this week on actual clearing prices for distressed cannabis assets.

The Rescheduling Docket

A ballot-box threat joins the implementation gap

Louisiana reportedly drops its challenge to federal rescheduling even as a Massachusetts repeal vote draws its first organized business opposition — and ALJ Derek Julius's recommended decision remains unissued more than ten weeks past the July 15 hearing close.

  • Aug 17, 2026 Post-hearing briefs filed with the DEA following the July 15 close of ALJ hearings on full Schedule I→III rescheduling.
  • Sep 14, 2026 Sens. Cortez Masto and Merkley reintroduce the SAFE Banking Act with a seven-member bipartisan cosponsor list.
  • Sep 17-18, 2026 TerrAscend moves to dismiss the DOJ's federal 280E clawback suit; Alabama's public-health board withdraws its objection to state-level rescheduling.
  • Sep 21, 2026 A shareholder suit against Dogtown Pioneers (a Washington hemp-beverage maker) challenges a roughly $4.6M IRS 280E assessment, arguing it was wrongly applied to raw cannabis inputs rather than finished sub-0.3%-THC beverages — a live test case for the hemp/280E boundary.
  • Sep 22, 2026 The Worcester Regional Chamber of Commerce opposes Massachusetts' Question 8, a Nov. 3 ballot measure to repeal the state's adult-use market; a Third Circuit panel separately rejects an attempt to use hemp legalization to invalidate a prior Pennsylvania marijuana conviction used for federal sentence enhancement, and a New Jersey federal judge upholds an arbitration award reinstating a worker fired over a positive THC test.
  • Sep 23, 2026 MJBizDaily reports Louisiana has dropped out of its federal rescheduling challenge (confirmed via headline indexing; full text paywalled). No ALJ recommended decision has been issued, and California's DCC continues to say the DEA has not provided states implementation guidance.

What's Next on the Docket

  1. ALJ Julius's recommended decision on full Schedule I→III rescheduling — still no fixed date, now more than ten weeks past the hearing close.
  2. Sep 24, 2026 (tomorrow) — Advanced Flower Capital's foreclosure auction of Justice Grown's Pennsylvania collateral.
  3. Oct 5, 2026 — DOJ's response due on TerrAscend's motion to dismiss the 280E clawback suit.
  4. Oct 6, 2026 — scheduled Advanced Flower Capital foreclosure auction of Justice Grown's New Jersey collateral.
  5. Nov 3, 2026 — up to six states could vote on marijuana- or psychedelics-related ballot measures, including Massachusetts' Question 8 to repeal its adult-use market.
  6. Nov 12, 2026 — converted/synthetic cannabinoids (delta-8, HHC, THC-O) lose federal hemp status; no extension applies.
  7. Dec 1, 2026 — Curaleaf's bid for Aurora Cannabis expires unless extended.
  8. Dec 11, 2026 — the enacted effective date for naturally-occurring hemp cannabinoids, under the 0.4mg-total-THC-per-container standard.
PGP takeaway Two separate risk tracks are now converging on the same client conversation: an implementation gap on the administrative side (DEA still hasn't briefed states on rescheduling mechanics) and, as of this week, a market-repeal ballot threat on the political side. Neither changes near-term 280E treatment for licensed medical operators, already in effect since the April 23 order — but both argue for modeling a longer, bumpier runway to full adult-use clarity than the headline "rescheduling is coming" framing suggests, especially for any deal with Massachusetts exposure.

Third Circuit rejects a hemp-law challenge to a marijuana sentence

A federal appeals panel declined to let hemp legalization be used to invalidate a prior Pennsylvania marijuana conviction that had been used to enhance a federal sentence. The Marijuana Herald

New Jersey judge upholds reinstatement of a worker fired over a THC test

A federal judge affirmed an arbitration award reinstating an employee terminated after a positive cannabinoid-metabolite test, consistent with New Jersey's bar on adverse action based solely on such tests. The Marijuana Herald

Hemp-beverage maker's investors challenge a $4.6M IRS assessment

Shareholders of Dogtown Pioneers, a Washington hemp-beverage company, filed suit arguing the IRS wrongly applied Section 280E to raw cannabis inputs rather than the finished, sub-0.3%-THC beverage product — a live test of the hemp/280E boundary. The Marijuana Herald

Louisiana reportedly drops its rescheduling challenge

MJBizDaily reports Louisiana has withdrawn from its legal challenge to federal marijuana rescheduling; the state separately maintains its own longer-standing state-level 280E exemption law. MJBizDaily

A cannabis MSO signs an LOI for a European tactical-drone reseller

IM Cannabis Corp. entered a non-binding letter of intent to acquire 51% of a Poland-based reseller of BlueBird Aero Systems drone products, funded via a registered direct offering; shares jumped roughly 103% pre-market on the news. PR Newswire

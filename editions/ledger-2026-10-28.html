Page One · The Big Story

MSOS Slips Back After a Two-Day Rally as Curaleaf Spells Out Its Case Against Aurora — Cresco's Redomicile Proxy Cutoff Passes, and Jushi's Q3 Print Looms After the Bell

The sector ETF gave back roughly 1% Wednesday afternoon after Tuesday's 2.5% bounce, with Glass House and Aurora — Tuesday's biggest gainers — among the day's biggest decliners; Curaleaf itemized its rebuttal of the Alberta Securities Commission's ATM ruling; and three separate MSO watchlist calendar events converge on the same 24 hours.

The two-session sector rally lost momentum Wednesday. MSOS traded near $3.99–$4.00 in the early afternoon (stockanalysis.com and Yahoo Finance, ~3:00–3:20pm ET), down roughly 1.0%–1.3% from Tuesday's $4.04 close, leaving the fund off about 3.4% on the week and a steep 22.7% month-to-date. The pullback was broad-based: Glass House Brands, Tuesday's leader at +10.75%, gave back 4.27% to $2.24, and Aurora Cannabis, up 5.90% Tuesday, fell 2.41% to $4.05. Canopy Growth (-2.63%), Trulieve (-2.25%) and Vireo Growth (-3.83%) also retreated; Verano was the lone gainer among reliably-quoted names, up 0.20% to $5.10 ahead of tomorrow's print. Planet 13 was a sharp outlier, up roughly 7-9% to about $0.081 — a move this desk attributes to arbitrage dynamics around its pending Vireo merger rather than an operating catalyst, since no new company disclosure was found.

Curaleaf used Tuesday's news cycle to put numbers behind chairman Boris Jordan's rebuttal of Monday's Alberta Securities Commission ruling. In a formal response, Curaleaf said its enhanced bid represents an 86% premium to Aurora's unaffected 30-day volume-weighted average price of roughly $2.75, accused Aurora's board of giving other unnamed parties diligence access it has denied Curaleaf, and noted Aurora halted its at-the-market program on Aug. 18 — shortly after Curaleaf first challenged it — undercutting Aurora's argument that the ATM program was unrelated to the bid fight. Aurora's own "actively exploring alternatives" posture traces to an Oct. 14 special-committee statement; no Organigram term sheet or exchange ratio has been filed by either side as of this edition.

Three MSO watchlist calendars converge on this 24-hour window: Cresco Labs' proxy cutoff for Friday's Delaware "TopCo" redomicile vote passed at noon CDT today with no result disclosed yet (the vote itself is Oct. 30); Jushi Holdings' Q3 2026 earnings call is set for after today's close, 4:30pm ET, with results not yet published as of this edition's compilation; and Verano's own Q3 print follows tomorrow, pre-market.

Why it matters to PGP Today's giveback is a reminder that this week's rally was concentrated in exactly the two names with the loudest negative headlines (Glass House, Aurora) and has partly reversed as quickly as it arrived — PGP should treat Tuesday's bounce as noise, not a trend, absent a fresh fundamental catalyst. On Curaleaf/Aurora, the itemized 86%-premium and diligence-access claims sharpen the proxy fight PGP should keep modeling as live into the Dec. 4 tender deadline. And with Jushi reporting tonight, Cresco's vote outcome due Friday, and Verano tomorrow, PGP should expect the next edition to carry three separate MSO catalysts at once.

Ascend's Vote Comes and Goes in Silence as the Cannabis Tape Posts Its Best Session in Weeks

MSOS jumps 3.31% to $4.99 on broad buying across Cresco, TerrAscend and Trulieve — but Ascend Wellness's own stock falls into its reverse-split special meeting with no vote result, ISS recommendation, or explanation for its Aug 20 spike ever disclosed.

The cannabis sector logged its strongest broad-based session in weeks on Friday. The AdvisorShares Pure US Cannabis ETF (NYSE Arca: MSOS) closed at $4.99, up 3.31% on volume of roughly 8.9 million shares — more than double Thursday's turnover — as Cresco Labs (+6.12%), TerrAscend (+5.73%), Trulieve (+7.16%), Verano (+3.42%) and Curaleaf (+3.21%) all advanced. No single confirmed news catalyst explains the move: no rescheduling development broke today, no hemp-bill vote occurred, and no fresh M&A landed. The rally instead coincides with two long-scheduled corporate events that were both due to conclude today without, as of this evening, any public disclosure of the outcome — Ascend Wellness's special stockholder meeting on its reverse stock split, held virtually at 11 a.m. ET, and Verano's national beverage brand Easy Landings reaching its fourth and final launch market, New Jersey.

Ascend's own stock did not join the rally: AAWH shares fell 5.67% to $0.3962 on light volume of 51,500 shares even as the sector around it climbed. No 8-K, press release, or ISS/Glass Lewis recommendation on the reverse split (proposed at a ratio between 1-for-10 and 1-for-50, tied to a planned U.S. exchange uplisting) had surfaced as of this evening's close — roughly seven hours after the meeting's scheduled start. That silence extends a run of unexplained Ascend trading action dating back to an intraday spike on Aug 20 that the company has never addressed. Verano's Easy Landings launch sits in a similar limbo: an Aug 20 release named today as the New Jersey on-sale date at Zen Leaf and partner dispensaries, but no coverage or company statement confirming the rollout had actually shipped by evening; VRNOF shares gained 3.42% to $6.05, in line with the broader tape.

Why it matters to PGP A green tape is not resolved event risk. Today's rally lifted nearly every name on this desk's watchlist, but it did nothing to answer two live diligence questions on Ascend — whether the reverse split clears, at what ratio, and what explains a month of unexplained stock action — or to confirm Verano's fourth beverage-market launch actually shipped. Treat both as open items until a dated filing lands; don't infer approval, completion, or investor sentiment from a sector-wide move that has no identified single cause.

Industry Headlines

A federal appeals court takes up Medicare's hemp-CBD coverage plan

Anti-marijuana organizations and a cannabis biopharmaceutical company are seeking to block a CMS initiative to cover hemp-derived CBD products — a new front adjacent to the rescheduling fight, with proceedings now scheduled. Marijuana Moment

Florida grand jury finds $10M misappropriated via Hope Florida Foundation

The panel found $8.5M of the total ultimately flowed to a political committee that fought the state's 2024 adult-use legalization initiative — a finding with implications for how future ballot fights get funded. Grand jury findings, reported Aug 27–28

Cresco's insurer heads for the exits as RICO claims mount

Palomar Excess & Surplus's coverage suit against Cresco Labs, seeking a declaration it owes no duty to defend the RICO/consumer-fraud class actions spanning 12 states, is cited as part of a broader pattern of insurers retreating from cannabis liability risk. Health Technology Net

Glass House marks two months on the Big Board with a bell-ringing

Management said on its Aug 13 earnings call that the team would be on the NYSE floor today, Aug 28, for a ceremonial bell-ringing following its June 30 uplisting; no post-ceremony confirmation had surfaced by evening. Earnings call transcript

Jushi now trades as JUSH after completing its Nevada redomicile

The move from British Columbia to Nevada, approved by shareholders June 24, took effect July 31 — a housekeeping step that also opened a relocated Beyond Hello dispensary in Belle Vernon, PA on Aug 20. GlobeNewswire

Kentucky's medical program passes the "halfway mark," governor says

Gov. Andy Beshear says the state's rollout is more than halfway toward full dispensary operability, positioning cannabis as an alternative to opioids in a state still building out its regulated market. Marijuana Moment

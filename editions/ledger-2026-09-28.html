Page One · The Big Story

Trulieve Locks Up Alien Labs and Connected for Florida and Texas as the Rest of the Sector Stays Dark

A perpetual, royalty-free brand license extends a four-year-old Florida exclusive into a Texas entry play — the only confirmed corporate release across a three-day window that otherwise left the priority desk and most of the MSO watchlist silent.

Trulieve announced Monday morning an exclusive master licensing agreement with Connected, giving it the rights to cultivate, manufacture and commercialize the Alien Labs and Connected brands — including genetics, trademarks and know-how — in Florida and Texas. The license is described as perpetual and royalty-free; neither company's release discloses a dollar figure, upfront fee or equity component, and this desk is not estimating one. In Florida the deal extends a supply relationship in place since 2022 into a broader, exclusive footprint with new categories and form factors; in Texas it functions as a market-entry vehicle, with products pending state licensure. Trulieve also picked up an option to negotiate rights in unnamed "additional expansion markets." CEO Kim Rivers framed it as premium-portfolio expansion; Connected founder Caleb Counts framed it as using Trulieve's distribution scale to extend the brand's reach.

Outside that one release, the weekend produced little. TerrAscend was the sector's standout mover — down 5.92% at Monday's close with no company-specific news identified behind it, the steepest decline among live-quoted names. The Advanced Flower Capital foreclosure auction of Justice Grown's Pennsylvania collateral, held last Thursday, still has no publicly disclosed result four days later. On the priority desk: Ascend Wellness produced no new release and its reverse-split ratio remains unset a full month after shareholder approval, even as the stock slipped 2.35% Monday on no identified catalyst; Verano stayed silent beyond last Tuesday's routine Q3 date notice, trading roughly flat near C$8.50; and Premier Group/PCF's California licenses were re-checked and remain active and clean, with no Modesto Bee, Turlock Journal or Santa Cruz Sentinel item in the window.

Why it matters to PGP The Trulieve-Connected structure — an exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free brand license rather than an acquisition, equity stake or supply-fee arrangement — is a template worth keeping on file for clients weighing how to secure premium genetics in a new limited-license market like Texas without the balance-sheet commitment of a purchase. It is also a signal that Texas is drawing real operator attention as a forward-looking expansion target ahead of any program buildout. Separately, the still-undisclosed AFC/Justice Grown auction result deserves a direct follow-up call rather than waiting on trade-press pickup — it remains the cleanest live read available this year on actual clearing prices for distressed cannabis retail/cultivation collateral.

PGP takeaway Nothing this week changes the "longer runway than the headline implies" read on federal rescheduling — the ALJ's silence is itself the data point, now stretching toward three months past the hearing close. The Massachusetts poll (71% opposed) remains the more actionable near-term signal, lowering tail risk on an outright market-repeal scenario heading into the Nov. 3 vote, though the downside case still needs to be modeled until the result is certified.

Page One · The Big Story

Vireo's Q2: Revenue Up 335%, and a Fifth Deal Joins the Roll-Up

GAAP revenue near $210M and $41.5M of adjusted EBITDA land alongside a confirmed Ohio entry and a definitive Cannabist asset deal — pushing Vireo toward 270 dispensaries across 16 states once everything closes.

Vireo Growth's second-quarter 2026 results, released this morning, showed GAAP revenue of $209.3M (+335% year-over-year) and pro forma revenue of $254.9M at a 45.5% gross margin, with adjusted EBITDA of $41.5M and $122.7M of cash on hand plus the new $65–105M asset-based credit facility closed Aug. 7. The release also confirmed two deals already on this desk's radar are now definitive: an asset purchase agreement for Cannabist Company positions that extends Vireo's Colorado footprint and adds entries in Massachusetts, New Jersey and Illinois, and the four-deal Ohio transaction announced July 31 — roughly $208M in Vireo stock for the FarmaceuticalRx group's eight dispensaries, targeted to close in Q4. Combined with the pending all-stock bid for Planet 13 (shareholder vote still pending) and the closed PharmaCann Colorado buy, management says the roll-up leaves Vireo operating in 16 states with roughly 270 dispensaries once every deal closes.

Separately, a correction to last session's coverage: the Senate's stopgap-funding vote that pushed the federal hemp-THC ban's toughest provisions to Dec. 11 passed 90-6, not 89-4 as first reported — after a separate 61-32 vote tabled a Sen. Ted Budd (R-NC) amendment that would have preserved the original Nov. 12 deadline. Also new: the Beverage Regulatory Parity Act was formally introduced Aug. 10 by Reps. Beth Van Duyne (R-TX) and Greg Landsman (D-OH), with specifics now public — a 5mg-per-serving THC cap, a three-tier alcohol-style distribution structure barring direct manufacturer-to-retailer sales, and an 8-cents-per-milligram federal excise tax. The Wine & Spirits Wholesalers Association has endorsed it.

Why it matters to PGP Vireo's roll-up is now five deals deep (Fluent, Pennsylvania, the Cannabist package, Ohio, and pending Planet 13) and funded almost entirely in stock and asset-based debt rather than cash — a template other well-capitalized MSOs could copy as distressed sellers keep surfacing (Cannabist's estate, family-owned regional chains like FarmaceuticalRx). On the hemp side, the excise-tax detail in the Beverage Regulatory Parity Act is the first concrete federal revenue mechanism proposed for hemp-derived THC — worth modeling into any beverage-category diligence alongside the Dec. 11 cliff still facing non-beverage SKUs.

The Rescheduling Docket

Schedule I → III: briefing period underway, and hemp beverages get a formal bill

The DEA record stays open for post-hearing briefs while Congress's carve-out for hemp beverages moves from a headline to an introduced bill — with a corrected vote count on the underlying delay.

  • Apr 23, 2026 DOJ order moves medical / FDA-approved marijuana to Schedule III; encourages Treasury to weigh 280E.
  • Jun 24–29, 2026 DEA ALJ hearings open; SAFER Banking reintroduced in both chambers (Senate Jun 24, House companion H.R. 9471 Jun 25).
  • Jul 15, 2026 Hearings conclude; record stays open for post-hearing briefs, due Aug 17.
  • Aug 3, 2026 Senate votes 61-32 to table a Budd (R-NC) amendment that would have kept the earlier Nov 12 hemp-ban deadline, then passes the underlying CR 90-6 — pushing the toughest hemp-THC restrictions to Dec 11. (Corrects the 89-4 figure carried in the prior edition.)
  • Aug 10, 2026 Reps. Van Duyne (R-TX) and Landsman (D-OH) formally introduce the Beverage Regulatory Parity Act — a 5mg THC/serving cap, a three-tier alcohol-style distribution structure, and an 8-cents-per-mg federal excise tax; the Wine & Spirits Wholesalers Association has endorsed it.
  • Jun–Aug, 2026 States keep maneuvering: Wyoming opts out (Jul 8), Louisiana withdraws its rescheduling objection (Jun), Alabama holds a public hearing (Jul 22) with a written-comment deadline of Aug 5 — no final State Board of Health decision reported yet — fragmenting 280E & interstate treatment.

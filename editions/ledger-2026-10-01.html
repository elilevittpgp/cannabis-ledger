Page One · The Big Story

SAFER Banking Clears a Senate Committee for the First Time Since 2013 — As the Tape Tries to Find a Floor

A 14-9 vote out of the Senate Banking Committee is the furthest cannabis banking reform has ever advanced in that chamber; it lands as MSOS claws back ~2% a day after its worst session of the year, while TerrAscend's "flat" Tuesday read unraveled into an 18% rout by the close.

The sector picked up a genuine, forward-looking catalyst on Sept. 27: the SAFER Banking Act passed the Senate Banking Committee 14-9, a bipartisan vote that marks the first time any version of the bill has cleared a Senate committee since it was first introduced in 2013. Committee Republicans including Sens. Cramer, Daines and Lummis joined Democrats in support; Chairman-aligned leadership has pledged a floor vote, though no date is scheduled. The bill would shield banks and credit unions that serve state-legal cannabis businesses from federal regulatory penalties — a structurally different lever than DEA rescheduling, and one this desk has flagged as more directly relevant to financing cost and availability for plant-touching operators.

That news is now colliding with a still-unsettled tape. MSOS traded at $4.63 as of mid-afternoon Oct. 1, up roughly 2.4% on the session, a partial bounce after Tuesday's ~13% rout tied to the DEA ALJ's stay of the rescheduling case. The bounce is uneven: delayed quotes show TerrAscend (TSNDF) closed Tuesday at $0.505, down 18.0% on heavy volume — a move this desk's Tuesday edition had read as "roughly flat" intraday before it reversed hard into the close, compounding a weak Q2 print. Ascend Wellness (AAWH) was up about 3.7% to $0.56 in the Thursday bounce; Verano (VRNO) last traded $5.39, still down roughly 10% from Tuesday's close.

Why it matters to PGP Treat SAFER Banking's committee passage and DEA rescheduling as two independent catalysts on two different clocks — a floor vote with no date set is not a near-term financing-cost event, and conflating the two in a model risks overstating near-term credit-availability upside. Separately, TerrAscend's reversal from "flat" to -18% in a single afternoon is a reminder that thinly traded, balance-sheet-stressed names carry idiosyncratic tail risk on top of sector beta — diligence on any OTC-listed MSO should model intraday dispersion, not just the ETF-level move.

The Rescheduling Docket

Schedule I → III: the stay holds, and the clock keeps running toward Oct 13

No DEA filing, ALJ ruling, or new docket activity has surfaced since Monday's stay — a genuine holding pattern with the government's response deadline now less than two weeks out.

  • Apr 23, 2026 DOJ order moves medical / FDA-approved marijuana to Schedule III; encourages Treasury to weigh 280E.
  • Jul 15, 2026 Broader Schedule I→III hearings conclude; ALJ Derek Julius later orders transcript corrections.
  • Aug 17, 2026 Post-hearing briefs due; full 2,533-page transcript published Aug 25.
  • Sep 23, 2026 GAO report finds DEA and FDA lack adequate written drug-scheduling procedures.
  • Sep 28-29, 2026 Three rescheduling opponents move to add the GAO report to the record; ALJ Julius stays the case and gives the DEA until Oct 13 to respond.
  • Oct 1, 2026 No DEA filing or ALJ ruling found — the stay remains in place with no new docket activity as of this edition.

What's Next on the Docket

  1. Oct 13, 2026 — DEA's response to the motion to reopen the record is due.
  2. ALJ Julius rules on whether to admit the GAO report and permit added briefing.
  3. If granted, a further briefing round precedes the recommended decision; if denied, the case resumes toward a decision on the existing record.
  4. A 20-day exceptions window, then the DEA Administrator's final call — with sell-side analysts (Cantor Fitzgerald) now modeling 1H 2027 as the more likely resolution window.
PGP takeaway A dead two weeks on the docket is itself informative: the market's violent Tuesday repricing (-13% MSOS) was a one-time adjustment to a longer timeline, not the start of a news-driven slide — Thursday's partial bounce supports that read. Models keyed to a 280E resolution should keep running on a 1H-2H 2027 base case, with Oct 13 as the next real checkpoint rather than a near-term catalyst to trade around.

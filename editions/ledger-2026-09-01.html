Two Deadlines, No Verdicts: The House Votes on Hemp Today, Aurora Owes Curaleaf an Answer Tomorrow

A two-thirds threshold stands between the Dec. 11 hemp-THC ban delay and passage; Aurora's formal response to Curaleaf's hostile bid is due within a day and had not been filed as this edition went to press.

The House was slated to take up the Senate-passed government-funding measure today, Sept. 1, under a suspension of the rules — a fast-track process that bars amendments but requires a two-thirds majority. The bill carries the delay of the federal hemp-THC redefinition ban from Nov. 12 to Dec. 11, 2026, already passed by the Senate 90-6 on Aug. 8. House Freedom Caucus chair and Agriculture Appropriations Subcommittee chair Rep. Andy Harris (R-Md.) told Punchbowl News Sunday night he would vote against the procedural motion, warning he and the Caucus "could torch the GOP leadership's agenda for the rest of the week" — though because suspension bars amendments, his only lever is opposing the bill outright, and Punchbowl's own reporting still expected passage by a wide margin. No roll-call result had been published as of this writing.

The second open item is closer to PGP's own lane. Aurora Cannabis's Directors' Circular — its formal, board-level recommendation on Curaleaf's hostile US$4.00-per-share bid (0.3463 Curaleaf shares plus $0.75 cash) — is due within 15 days of the bid's Aug. 18 commencement, putting the deadline at essentially today or tomorrow, Sept. 1–2. As of this writing, no circular has been filed; Aurora's only public statements remain its Aug. 24 rebuttal calling the bid opportunistic and disputing Curaleaf's characterizations. Aurora shares trade around $4.04 on the Nasdaq — essentially at the bid's implied value, a sign the market isn't yet pricing a competing offer or a clean rejection. Elsewhere, the tape's most notable single-name move was unexplained: Trulieve traded up as much as 5.9% intraday to $12.19 with no company release found to account for it. All three of the desk's priority names — Ascend, Premier Group/PCF, and Verano — were quiet.

Why it matters to PGP Neither open item is a formality. A failed or stripped hemp delay would pull the Dec. 11 cliff forward in market psychology even if the statutory date doesn't move, repricing hemp-THC and beverage exposure overnight. And Aurora's circular — whenever it lands — is the first hard signal on deal mechanics: a firm rejection pushes Curaleaf into a genuinely contested tender through Dec. 1, while a softer, "evaluating alternatives" posture would reprice the situation as a probable friendly resolution. Treat both as live through the close of business today.

Industry Headlines

DEA's Colorado questionnaire shows what Schedule III compliance will actually require

A 26-question form now used with Colorado medical-cannabis applicants probes suppliers, product types, interstate purchasing, synthetic cannabinoids, labeling and disposal — the clearest look yet at DEA's registration bar for state-licensed dispensaries. Cannabis Business Times

Oklahoma extraction-facility owner charged with murder after blast kills two

Ryan Bassham, owner of Solos Extracts LLC, was charged with two counts of first-degree murder after an Aug 27 explosion in Sand Springs; the unlicensed facility had reportedly been ordered to cease operations a week earlier after failing a fire inspection. Washington Times · AP

Headset to pay $1.01M over a PPP loan federal prosecutors say it never qualified for

The Seattle cannabis-data analytics firm settles a False Claims Act suit alleging its marijuana-industry client base made it ineligible for a 2021 PPP loan later forgiven — no wrongdoing admitted, paid over four years. The Marijuana Herald · DOJ (W.D. Wash.)

High Tide adds three Canna Cabana stores, holds its 20-store guidance

New Ontario and Saskatchewan locations opening Sep 2-5 bring the chain to 232 Canadian stores, funded from internal cash flow with no dilution disclosed. PR Newswire

Endovia Health Sciences to submit a CBD/THC epilepsy drug to the FDA this week

The company says rescheduling momentum supports a pre-submission conference for CannEpil and that it is reviewing cannabinoid and beverage assets for acquisition. StockTitan

Nevada's new per-location cannabis tax permit deadline hits today

Wholesalers, retailers and consumption lounges had until Sep 1 to obtain permits under SB 41 (2025) or risk suspension by the Cannabis Compliance Board. The Marijuana Herald

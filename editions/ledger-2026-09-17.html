Page One · The Big Story

Curaleaf Puts Boris Jordan In Front of Aurora's Shareholders — the Referee Still Hasn't Ruled

A live Sept 17 Q&A caps a three-day escalation from regulatory complaint to direct-to-shareholder campaign; the Alberta Securities Commission has yet to set a hearing date on the underlying ATM dispute.

Curaleaf's hostile bid for Aurora Cannabis reached its most public phase yet this morning. Having sent Aurora shareholders a direct letter on Sept 15 reiterating its offer — 0.3463 Curaleaf shares plus US$0.75 cash per Aurora share, a stated 45% premium to the unaffected 30-day VWAP — and disclosed a shareholder "brochure" filing on Sept 16, Chairman and CEO Boris Jordan held a live, webcast shareholder Q&A at 10:30 a.m. ET today aimed squarely at Aurora's retail holders. As of this edition's early-afternoon compile, no wrap coverage or transcript of the session had surfaced, and the Alberta Securities Commission has neither scheduled a hearing nor ruled on Curaleaf's Sept 14 application to halt Aurora's at-the-market share program. Aurora's board continues to recommend shareholders take no action on the bid, which expires Dec 1.

Elsewhere, TerrAscend's response to the DOJ's first-of-its-kind federal 280E refund-clawback suit is now due tomorrow, Sept 18, with no filing yet as of this edition. Cresco Labs added Edward Tilly — the former Chairman and CEO of Cboe Global Markets — to its board effective Sept 18, joining the Audit Committee, a governance hire notable for bringing three-plus decades of exchange-operator experience onto an MSO board. On the rescheduling docket, DEA Chief ALJ Derek Julius still has not issued a recommended decision on full Schedule I-to-III reclassification, now roughly nine weeks past the July 15 close of hearings, even as DEA published the complete hearing transcript earlier this month as a procedural step. Ascend, Verano and Premier Group/PCF all stayed quiet again.

Why it matters to PGP A hostile bidder pivoting from a stalled regulatory complaint to a live retail-investor roadshow is a template worth filing for future contested cross-border cannabis takeovers — it signals Curaleaf doesn't expect the ASC alone to move the needle before Dec 1. Separately, a former exchange chief joining Cresco's board is the kind of governance signal diligence teams should flag on any MSO with uplisting ambitions, regardless of whether it's announced as such.

The Rescheduling Docket

Nine weeks past the hearing close, still no ALJ decision

DEA publishes the full hearing transcript as a procedural step; a Supreme Court petition on cannabis-licensing residency preferences opens a new legal front adjacent to rescheduling.

  • Aug 17, 2026 Post-hearing briefs filed with the DEA following the July 15 close of ALJ hearings on full Schedule I→III rescheduling.
  • Aug 18, 2026 A bipartisan House group (Titus, Joyce, Omar, Steube) sends a letter to the administration seeking answers on rescheduling mechanics by Sept 30.
  • Sep 8-9, 2026 GAO publishes GAO-26-107498 on cannabis banking access; the D.C. Circuit denies a stay motion against the DOJ's April 2026 Schedule III order.
  • Sep 14, 2026 Sens. Cortez Masto and Merkley reintroduce the SAFE Banking Act with a seven-member bipartisan cosponsor list.
  • Sep 2026 DEA publishes the complete rescheduling-hearing transcript, a procedural step ahead of ALJ Julius's recommended decision.
  • Sep 17, 2026 No ALJ recommended decision yet — roughly nine weeks past the hearing close; separately, a Supreme Court petition asks the justices to decide whether state residency-based cannabis-licensing preferences violate the dormant Commerce Clause.

What's Next on the Docket

  1. ALJ Julius's recommended decision on full Schedule I→III rescheduling — still no fixed date.
  2. Sep 18, 2026 — TerrAscend's response due in the DOJ's federal 280E clawback suit.
  3. Sep 24 / Oct 6, 2026 — scheduled Advanced Flower Capital foreclosure auctions of Justice Grown's PA and NJ collateral.
  4. Sep 30, 2026 — deadline for the administration to answer lawmakers' rescheduling-mechanics letter.
  5. Nov 12, 2026 — converted/synthetic cannabinoids (delta-8, HHC, THC-O) lose federal hemp status; no extension applies.
  6. Dec 1, 2026 — Curaleaf's bid for Aurora Cannabis expires unless extended.
  7. Dec 11, 2026 — the enacted effective date for naturally-occurring hemp cannabinoids, under the 0.4mg-total-THC-per-container standard.
PGP takeaway The transcript's publication is a procedural checkpoint, not a signal on timing or outcome — underwrite on the assumption that the ALJ's recommendation remains undated through year-end. The new Supreme Court licensing petition is a separate but adjacent risk: a residency-preference ruling would reach past cannabis into every state's social-equity licensing framework, and is worth tracking independently of the Schedule III fight.
